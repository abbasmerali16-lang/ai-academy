    Questions

    Good to know

    Do I need maths or coding?

    Not to start. Level 1 uses plain English and everyday examples. Python and maths begin gently, from zero, in Level 2, and every later level builds on what came before.

    How long does each level take?

    Level 1 is about three hours. Later levels are a little longer, about four to five hours each, because there is code to try. One lesson a day gets you through a level in just over two weeks.

    Where do the facts come from?

    Each lesson is written fresh and checked against respected courses, textbooks and official guidance, such as Elements of AI, Russell and Norvig, the OECD and the EU AI Act. Every lesson lists its sources at the end.

    Can I jump to a later level?

    Yes. Every level that is ready is open to you. Use "Your road to mastery" or the menu to switch levels. Starting in order is best, because each level builds on the one before.

    Where is my progress saved?

    In this browser on this device. If you switch device or clear your browser data, you'll start fresh.

    What if I fail a quiz?

    Nothing bad happens. Re-read the lesson and try again as many times as you like.
